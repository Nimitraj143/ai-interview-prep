FAQ

Common questions

How are my answers scored?

Each answer is sent to the Gemini AI model, which returns a score out of 10 and short feedback. Treat it as practice guidance, not a hiring verdict.

What if the AI is not available?

The app falls back to a general set of interview questions and a basic score, so you can still practice. Feedback will be less specific in that case.

How do I get the most relevant questions?

Paste your resume text in the box on the resume step. The more detail about projects and skills you include, the more specific the questions will be.

Is the ATS score official?

No. Real systems such as Workday or Greenhouse do not publish a score. The ATS checker is an estimate based on keywords, structure and formatting. Use the missing keywords and tips, and do not chase the exact number.

Can I skip a question?

Yes. Skipped questions are not counted in your average score and are marked as skipped in the report.

Ready for a practice round?
